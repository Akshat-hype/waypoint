# Waypoint — Product Requirements Document

## Overview

Waypoint is an offline-first personal travel companion application that helps users organize, manage, and preserve their travel experiences in one platform.

The application combines:
- Trip planning
- Expense tracking
- Itinerary management
- Photo/video memories
- Travel analytics
- Notes and journaling

---

# Product Vision

To build a modern travel management platform that works as a personal travel memory vault and planning assistant.

---

# Objectives

## Primary Objectives
- Centralize all travel information
- Enable offline-first functionality
- Simplify expense tracking
- Preserve travel memories

## Secondary Objectives
- Support future AI integrations
- Provide cloud synchronization
